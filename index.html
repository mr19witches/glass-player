Connect your accounts

Glass Player shows lyrics and controls the Spotify app on this PC, so keep Spotify open. Controlling playback needs Spotify Premium.

  1. In your Spotify developer app, add the redirect URI http://127.0.0.1:8888/callback and paste its Client ID below.
  2. For word-synced lyrics, create an application at developers.spicylyrics.org and paste its secret key. Without it you get line-synced lyrics.
